## The scale of growth

According to CoinGecko's report on tokenized stocks, the sector's active market capitalization grew from $965 million at the start of 2026 to roughly $4 billion by September 9. Monthly transfer volume of tokenized stocks over the 30 days ending August 29 rose by more than 415% — to $29.5 billion.

## Where the market is concentrated

Despite the rapid growth, turnover rests on a very narrow set of players: bStocks and Robinhood together accounted for 87.8% of tracked trading volume for the month, and at the network level BNB Chain and Robinhood Chain hold 88.2% of the combined share. That means the tokenized-stock market is so far less a distributed infrastructure than a few competing platform ecosystems.

## Spot isn't the main thing

The bulk of volume isn't generated by spot trades but by perpetual futures on tokenized stocks: their turnover was $376.3 billion versus $7.5 billion in spot — a 209x rise over 12 months. That suggests tokenized stocks are being used first and foremost for speculative leverage rather than for actual ownership of the underlying asset.
