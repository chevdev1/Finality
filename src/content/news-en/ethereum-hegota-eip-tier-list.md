## What Hegotá is and why the ranking is news

Hegotá is the working name for the Ethereum upgrade that is to follow Glamsterdam (which itself hasn't even reached mainnet yet — activation has slipped to the fourth quarter of 2026). Previously, teams in the Ethereum Foundation's protocol cluster published their views on upgrade candidates separately, on behalf of each team. The September 7 post is the first time the cluster has issued a single, agreed-upon assessment of all 62 proposals at once.

## How the grades were counted

Each of the 12 contributors who voted (out of 16 invited across the cluster's nine teams) independently graded every EIP on a scale: S = 4 points, A = 3, B = 2, C = 1, D/declined = 0. That produced 397 individual grades across 62 proposals — an average of 6.4 votes each, after which contested items were debated by the team live. This differs markedly from the practice where the fate of a given EIP effectively rests on the opinion of a single tech lead.

## What made it and what didn't

Only two proposals earned the top S grade — "must ship": EIP-7805 (FOCIL, a consensus-layer mechanism) and EIP-8141 (Frame Transactions, on the execution layer). Roughly three more dozen proposals were spread across the A/B/C tiers — from "likely to be included" to "below the line but not closed" — and two proposals are marked "TBD": the decision is deferred until mainnet data is available after Glamsterdam launches. 28 proposals were declined outright, and the post's authors gave a reason for each rejection rather than stopping at the bare fact of it.

## What this means for the reader

The list of EIPs itself will tell a non-developer little. What matters more is the fact itself: the more formalized and public the procedure for selecting changes to the Ethereum protocol, the less room there is for the network's future to hinge on the sway of a single team or developer. Hegotá's release date hasn't been announced — the upgrade follows Glamsterdam, which itself doesn't yet have an exact mainnet activation date.
