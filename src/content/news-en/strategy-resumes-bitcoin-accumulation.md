## The pause is over

Strategy (formerly MicroStrategy), the largest public holder of bitcoin, resumed buying after a ten-week break — a stretch that looked uncharacteristically long for a company whose business model is effectively built on continuously growing its position. The new purchase — 4,603 BTC for $369.7 million at an average price of $80,318 per coin — lifted the total treasury to 845,050 BTC, acquired for roughly $63.73 billion in all since the strategy began.

## How the purchase was funded

Like almost all previous rounds of accumulation, this purchase was funded by issuing new shares rather than from the cash flow of the company's operating business (originally an enterprise-software developer). That's a core part of Strategy's model: the capital market effectively funds the bitcoin purchase in exchange for shares of a company that, in turn, trades as a leveraged proxy for bitcoin itself.

## The scale of the position

845,050 BTC is roughly 4% of all bitcoin that will ever be in circulation (a maximum of 21 million coins). Such concentration in the hands of a single public company is itself a significant structural fact for the market: it makes Strategy not just a large holder but a factor whose own decisions to buy or potentially sell can move the market noticeably.

## Market reaction

The company's shares responded with a sharp rise: from around $92 in mid-August to $144.82 by September 3 — a gain of about 57% in under three weeks. Analysts who initiated coverage of the company with a "buy" rating explain it not only by the rise in the bitcoin price but by the thesis that Strategy may outperform bitcoin itself in a bull-market phase through yield strategies layered on top of the base position — although that thesis remains analysts' conjecture, not a confirmed result.
