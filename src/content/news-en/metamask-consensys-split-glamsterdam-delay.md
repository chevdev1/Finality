## What happened with MetaMask

On September 9, the MetaMask wallet, one of the most recognizable products in the Ethereum ecosystem, formally separated from Consensys, the company that had developed it since launch. Consensys said it plans to complete the split of its consumer business (which includes MetaMask) and its institutional arm, focused on infrastructure for banks and corporations, by the end of the year.

## What about Glamsterdam's timing

At the same time, the timing of Ethereum's next major protocol upgrade, Glamsterdam, has also shifted. Mainnet activation has moved to the fourth quarter of 2026, and the fork of the Sepolia testnet, where the upgrade is tested before mainnet, is set for September 28.

## Why they matter together

The MetaMask separation and the protocol-upgrade delay are formally unrelated events, but both show the Ethereum ecosystem entering a phase of structural maturation: products for retail users and institutional infrastructure are diverging onto separate development and governance tracks, and the timing of protocol changes is increasingly set by the caution of testing rather than a fixed calendar.
