## What PYUSDx does

PYUSDx isn't a new stablecoin but an infrastructure tool: it lets third-party companies issue their own branded stablecoins backed by PayPal USD reserves. That sets PayPal's approach apart from direct issuance — instead of competing for users with its own token, the company offers infrastructure for other people's tokens.

## Who has already joined

On launch day, three projects were already running on PYUSDx with combined transaction volume above $100 million — a rare case where new financial infrastructure shows measurable volume from day one, not just an announcement of intent.

## How this relates to paying with crypto

Separately from PYUSDx, PayPal already runs Pay with Crypto: verified merchants in the US can accept payments in around 100 cryptocurrencies — including PYUSD, USDC, and USDT — from buyers worldwide, with the amount arriving to the merchant in dollars on their PayPal balance, without having to hold the crypto asset itself. The promotional fee is 0.99% of the transaction amount, after which it's the standard 1.5%.
