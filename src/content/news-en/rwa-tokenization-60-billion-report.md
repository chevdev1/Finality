## Why Treasuries lead

According to CoinGecko's 2026 State of Tokenization report, tokenized US government debt is the only asset class its authors call truly ready for scale: 99% of issues are structured so they can move across public blockchain rails rather than being locked in closed internal registries. Among the largest products are Circle's USYC, Ondo Finance's USDY, Franklin Templeton's iBENJI, and WisdomTree's WTGXX.

## The rest of the market is still catching up

Private credit remains the largest category of tokenized RWAs by volume, but the report notes that about 56% of the stated RWA value actually sits idle without active use, and 97% of the value of tokenized assets is inaccessible to retail investors in the US — institutional demand still outpaces retail by an order of magnitude.

## What this means for the crypto market

RWA growth is directly tied to the CLARITY bill: the clearer the rules for tokenized financial instruments, the more willingly institutional issuers bring new asset classes on-chain beyond Treasuries.
