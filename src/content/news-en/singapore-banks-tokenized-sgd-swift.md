## What exactly happened

Singapore's three largest banks — DBS, OCBC, and UOB — carried out live domestic payments in Singapore dollars, using tokenized deposits and Swift's shared blockchain ledger to exchange payment messages among themselves. It is the first time three Singaporean banks have executed interbank transactions this way, rather than merely testing the technology in an isolated environment.

## How settlement works technically

Each bank runs its own tokenized-deposit infrastructure — that is, a digital representation of customer deposits on its internal ledger. When an interbank payment occurs, the resulting obligations are recorded as tokenized deposit liabilities in each bank's system, then reconciled and netted on the shared Swift ledger, while final settlement still goes through existing banking settlement infrastructure. Shared-ledger technology is used here not to replace traditional settlement but for faster messaging and reconciliation between banks.

## Part of a broader Swift initiative

The project isn't an isolated Singapore initiative: it follows Swift's July announcement that its blockchain ledger was ready for first use, when 17 banks on six continents said they were ready to carry out similar live transactions with tokenized deposits. The Singapore pilot focuses on domestic SGD payments — the next logical step for such infrastructure is cross-border settlement, where the potential gain from bypassing traditional correspondent-bank chains is markedly higher.

## Why it matters

A shared blockchain ledger supporting interbank settlement can potentially run around the clock, not only within the processing windows of traditional systems — a key difference from familiar settlement mechanisms tied to banking hours and clearing cycles. For the industry, it's another signal that the largest traditional banks are testing tokenization not as an experiment on the periphery but as part of core settlement infrastructure — in parallel with the story of the 21-bank consortium preparing its own dollar stablecoin.
