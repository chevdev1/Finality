## A volatile start to September

September started nervously for spot bitcoin ETFs: on September 1 the funds recorded a net outflow of about $236 million, on September 2 an inflow of $101 million, and on September 4 another $175 million. But the peak came on September 3: $731 million in net inflows in a single day, the best result since January 14.

## Why IBIT sets the tone

Of the $731 million that entered the funds on September 3, $454 million — more than 60% — went to BlackRock's iShares Bitcoin Trust (IBIT). Such concentration of inflows in one product is the usual picture for the spot bitcoin ETF market, where IBIT has consistently led competitors in new money since launch.

## What this means for the market

Sharp swings between inflows and outflows within a few days usually point to institutional investors reacting selectively to macro data and Fed meetings, rather than to retail demand, which moves more slowly. For the industry, it's a signal that spot ETFs have become one of the fastest indicators of institutional sentiment toward bitcoin.
