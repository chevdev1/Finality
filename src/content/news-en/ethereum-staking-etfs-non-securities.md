## What the regulators' clarification changed

The joint interpretive release from the SEC and CFTC of March 17, 2026 classified Ethereum staking rewards as falling outside the definition of a security. Until then, uncertainty over the status of staking rewards had for more than a year held issuers back from launching ETFs with real staking inside the fund, rather than just spot exposure to ETH.

## What's already launched

Two Ethereum staking ETFs are currently operating in the US: Grayscale's ETHE (since October 2025) and BlackRock's ETHB (since March 2026), with five more issuers awaiting approval of their applications. Gross staking yield on the network is 3.1–3.3% a year; after fund fees and custody costs, net yield for fund shareholders is 1.9–2.6% a year.

## Scale on the network

The staked share of ETH in total supply has risen to 34.23%, the market capitalization of staking has exceeded $77 billion, and the total staked coins amount to about 41 million ETH. In parallel, a proposal is being discussed on the Ethereum research forum to let validators direct up to 10% of their rewards toward funding the ecosystem's infrastructure and public goods.
