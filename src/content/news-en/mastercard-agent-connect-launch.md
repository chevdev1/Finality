## What Agent Connect solves

Agent Connect gives merchants, AI agents, shopping platforms, and payment providers a single integration point instead of a separate connection to each AI platform. Through it, a merchant gets one path for displaying products, building a cart, and processing payment with customer confirmation — regardless of which AI platform the shopper started the purchase on.

## Who has already joined

The initial network operates in the US. Among the first partners are Bemobi, Getnet, Glance, Global Payments, Planet, Network International, Nexi, Samsung, Trip.com, Vodafone Egypt, Wizard, and Wompi.

## How it relates to Mastercard Agent Suite

Agent Connect complements the earlier Mastercard Agent Suite — a set of services for bringing agentic AI into businesses' everyday operations. Within it, Mastercard also partners with Anthropic, adding Claude models for post-purchase support — for example, order tracking and processing returns — and continues work with other AI companies, including OpenAI, on scenarios for agent-authorized purchases at merchants.
