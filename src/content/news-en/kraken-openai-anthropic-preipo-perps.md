## What exactly was launched

The OpenAI (ticker PF_OPENAIXUSD) and Anthropic (PF_ANTHROPICXUSD) contracts are perpetual futures with no expiration date, leverage of up to 5x, and several types of margin collateral. The key feature is synthetic pricing: Kraken uses its own valuation mechanism, Kraken PreMarket Synthetic, which computes the price from trading activity within the perpetuals market itself rather than pulling it from external valuations of the companies' shares on the secondary pre-IPO market.

## What it doesn't give the holder

An open position in these contracts doesn't create a right to shares, a vote at shareholder meetings, a right to dividends, or any stake in OpenAI or Anthropic — it is a purely speculative instrument on market expectations about the companies' valuations, not a way to become a co-owner.

## Who can't access it

Kraken has fully excluded customers from the US, the European Economic Area, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand from access to the product — the jurisdictions with the strictest regulation of derivatives on private companies. That limits the product's audience to users in jurisdictions with lighter oversight of retail derivatives.
