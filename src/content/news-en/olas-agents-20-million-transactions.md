## What Olas counts

Olas is a protocol on which autonomous AI agents register: they trade on DeFi markets through the BabyDegen module, create and resolve prediction markets through Olas Predict, and interact with each other and with the protocol's marketplace. The metric of 19,953,588 transactions as of August 14 covers all such agents' actions across eight networks — Ethereum, Gnosis, Base, Mode, Optimism, Celo, Arbitrum, and Polygon — with the largest share (by the protocol's own data) falling on Gnosis.

## A number that doesn't match intuition

Setting two figures from the same protocol material side by side creates an obvious contrast: almost 20 million transactions — and yet the Olas marketplace's lifetime turnover is $107,808.60, and the fees collected by the protocol are $546.46 at the 15% rate introduced in the second quarter of 2026. That's not a contradiction or an error — they're simply different things: the overwhelming majority of the 19.9 million transactions don't pass through the marketplace and generate no turnover for it, but belong to other kinds of agent activity (interactions with each other, actions in Olas Predict, BabyDegen's DeFi operations).

## How the protocol itself explains the gap

In its own piece, titled "Approaching 20 million Olas agent transactions: spam or something else?", the protocol doesn't give the share of transactions that carried real economic value versus insignificant or technical operations. Instead of a quantitative breakdown, Olas appeals to transparency: every transaction is "traceable to an on-chain record that anyone can verify" — that is, the argument rests on the verifiability of the fact of the transaction itself, not on its economic weight.

## How to read this figure

For a reader assessing the real scale of the "AI agent economy" in crypto, the number of on-chain transactions is by itself a poor measure without comparison to dollar turnover: 20 million actions against a marketplace turnover in the six figures is more evidence of a large number of small, possibly test or low-value operations than of a mature market. That fits a broader observation about the industry: the amount of AI-agent activity in crypto is growing faster than unambiguous evidence that this activity creates comparable economic value.
