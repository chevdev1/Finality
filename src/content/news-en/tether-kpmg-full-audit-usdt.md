## The difference between an attestation and a full audit

Until August 2026, all of Tether's reserve disclosure rested solely on quarterly attestation reviews by BDO — these confirm a snapshot of the balance sheet on a specific date but do not constitute a full audited set of financial statements. Bringing in KPMG changes that: the audit covers Tether International's full financial statements for the year ended December 31, 2025, with PwC assisting on internal preparation for the review.

## Why it's happening now

A full audit from a Big Four firm is expected to satisfy the reserve-verification and reporting requirements the GENIUS Act places on foreign stablecoin issuers wanting to register in the US. In January 2026, Tether had already launched USAT — a stablecoin designed specifically to comply with the GENIUS Act.

## How Circle looks against this

Circle has historically disclosed USDC reserves more often: a weekly publication of reserve composition with a monthly review by a Big Four firm — a more frequent disclosure cycle than Tether's prior quarterly rhythm. Tether's move to a full annual audit narrows that transparency gap but doesn't eliminate the difference in disclosure frequency between the two largest issuers of dollar stablecoins.
