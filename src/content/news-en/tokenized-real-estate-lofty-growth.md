## Why real estate and credit are growing faster than bonds

Tokenized US Treasuries still lead by volume — about $9 billion issued as of mid-2026 — but it is private credit and real estate that show accelerated growth, because tokenization gives them something these assets never had before: liquidity for historically illiquid, high-yield instruments.

## What's happening in tokenized real estate

Platform self-reports put the tokenized real-estate market at $438.88 million across 64 assets in 11 countries as of March 2026, while public registries record only about $226 million across 105 assets actually distributed on-chain — the gap between claimed and verifiable remains significant. Against that backdrop, Lofty stands out: the platform holds $100.5 million in total value across more than 180 properties in 21 US states on the Algorand blockchain, with a $50 entry threshold, more than 40,000 cumulative investors, and an average yield of 11%.

## What this means for asset owners

The main story of 2026 isn't only about investors but about asset owners: tokenization is becoming a real capital-raising strategy that gives access to a global pool of investors, reduces reliance on bank financing, speeds up deal closings, and creates a more transparent capital structure for properties that used to be financed exclusively through local lenders.
