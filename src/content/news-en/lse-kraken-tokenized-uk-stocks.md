## What exactly they agreed to do

London Stock Exchange Group — one of the oldest and largest stock exchanges in the world — is partnering with Payward, the parent company of the Kraken crypto exchange, to tokenize shares traded in London. The exchange's 100 largest listed companies will launch as xStocks in 2027; the first tokenized London shares are expected on Kraken and other xStocks alliance venues within weeks of the announcement.

## Who can't access it yet

Despite the exchange's British origins, xStocks on London shares will not initially be available to UK investors — the product is aimed at overseas demand from crypto-exchange users rather than the domestic market. That is a typical model for tokenized stocks at the moment: access is structured to sidestep local regulatory restrictions on retail derivatives rather than to widen access for local investors.

## What could follow

Besides launching through Kraken, Payward and the LSE are exploring a more ambitious option — native share tokens issued directly through the exchange's own infrastructure, with the same rights and full fungibility as ordinary shares on the exchange. If realized, it would be one of the first cases where a major traditional stock exchange issues a tokenized version of its listings as a first-class product of its own, rather than leaving it to a third-party crypto issuer.
