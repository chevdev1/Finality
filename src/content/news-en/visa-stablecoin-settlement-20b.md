## What exactly grew

Visa reported that the annual pace of stablecoin settlement on its payment network has passed $20 billion — more than 15x growth year over year. The number of stablecoin-linked card programs on the Visa network has passed 160, and payment volume on them has grown almost 200% over the same period. That means Visa's stablecoin settlement is moving from the pilot stage to mass use.

## Why on-chain lending is needed

Issuers of stablecoin cards must cover their obligations to Visa for processed transactions every day. To simplify that, Visa is launching an on-chain credit line with Credit Coop that lets issuers get financing against day-to-day settlement and includes a 7-day card settlement cycle. Since 2023, more than $2.5 billion in settlement volume has been financed through this system without a single default — Visa cites this as proof of the mechanism's reliability ahead of broader rollout.

## Context

Visa's growth coincides in time with PayPal's PYUSDx launch and U.S. Bank's live USBDC pilot — three major payments-industry players are building stablecoin infrastructure in parallel, even before the final licensing rules under the GENIUS Act take effect.
