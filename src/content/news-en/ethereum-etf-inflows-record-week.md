## The best week since launch

US spot Ethereum ETFs recorded $824.41 million in net inflows for the week of August 24 to 28 — the strongest five-day result since they launched. Inflows were steady across the days of the week: $115.57 million on Monday, $179.80 million on Tuesday, $192.35 million on Wednesday, $234.51 million on Thursday, and $102.18 million on Friday — that is, not a sharp spike on one day but steady demand all week.

## Who drove the inflows

Of the $824 million for the week, $567 million went to BlackRock's ETHA, bringing the fund's assets under management to $12.74 billion. Fidelity's FETH added $96.5 million, lifting its assets to $2.27 billion. Combined assets of all spot Ethereum ETFs reached $15.23 billion — 5.2% of Ethereum's entire market capitalization, now concentrated in exchange-traded funds.

## The streak broke a week later

Inflows continued into the following week (August 31 to September 4), adding another $218.40 million and lifting the two-week total above $1 billion. But on September 9 the funds posted a net outflow of $24 million for the first time in a long while — small next to the previous weeks, but marking the end of the rising streak rather than a one-day blip.

## Why it matters

Institutional demand through exchange-traded funds remains the main channel through which large capital enters Ethereum, bypassing direct ownership or self-run staking. The rise of ETH ETFs' share of the network's capitalization to 5.2% is a notable quantitative marker: for comparison, Ethereum has never before had such a concentration of supply in regulated investment products.
