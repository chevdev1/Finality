    q_en: "What is the CLARITY Act?"
    a_en: "It's the US crypto market-structure bill. It's meant to divide oversight of the market between the SEC and the CFTC; regulators have clashed over jurisdiction for years, notably over the spot market for crypto commodities, where the CFTC's authority is limited today."

    a_en: "The Senate held a procedural (cloture) vote and fell short of the required 60 votes. The exact tally differs by outlet: The Crypto Times, citing the official floor record, reports 49 yes to 50 no; CoinGape gives an unofficial 46 to 43. All sources agree the gap from 60 wasn't close."

    q_en: "Is the bill dead?"

    q_en: "What held up agreement?"
    a_en: "Three issues stayed open: ethics rules for lawmakers who own digital assets, illicit-finance rules, and whether stablecoin issuers may pay holders rewards resembling yield."
